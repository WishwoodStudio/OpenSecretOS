# PE-003 --- Shot Duration vs. Perceived Dynamics

**Category:** Visual Language\
**Status:** Hypothesis (not yet validated)\
**Episode:** Luxury Destruction\
**Evidence source:** First production renders (Blocks A and B, Seedance
2.0 Mini)

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Observation

Blocks A and B each depict one continuous cinematic action over
approximately 15 seconds.

Viewed in isolation, both shots:

-   feel premium;
-   match the intended Open Secret documentary aesthetic;
-   provide clean space for typography and overlays;
-   do not appear visually incorrect.

However, they also maintain a largely continuous composition and camera
move throughout the block.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Hypothesis

A sequence of long uninterrupted AI shots **may** feel slower than
expected in short-form vertical video **unless** sufficient editorial
motion is introduced through:

-   typography,
-   diagrams,
-   VO pacing,
-   music,
-   or editing rhythm.

This is currently a hypothesis only.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Evidence Status

Current evidence is **insufficient**.

Only isolated generated blocks have been reviewed.

No assembled episode exists yet.

Therefore it is impossible to judge perceived pacing in the actual
viewer experience.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Validation Plan

Validate only after the first complete assembly of *Luxury Destruction*.

Questions to answer:

1.  Does the episode feel visually static?
2.  Do typography and diagrams provide enough perceived motion?
3.  Do viewers experience any pacing drop during long continuous AI
    shots?
4.  If yes, is the cause:
    -   shot duration,
    -   editing,
    -   typography,
    -   VO pacing,
    -   or something else?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Current Recommendation

**No production change.**

Do **not** shorten shots.

Do **not** redesign the Director Package.

Do **not** change the visual language.

Continue production through completion of all remaining blocks and
assemble the first complete episode.

Only then decide whether shot duration should become a Visual Identity
or Production Playbook guideline.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Why this document exists

This document records an observed production hypothesis so it is not
forgotten or prematurely promoted into project doctrine.

Future changes should be based on completed production evidence rather
than isolated renders.
