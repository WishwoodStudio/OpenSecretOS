# PE-003 --- First Full Episode Visual Findings

**Status:** Production Evidence\
**Episode:** Luxury Destruction\
**Stage:** First fully assembled review (pre-publication)

## Purpose

Capture observations that emerged only after reviewing the first
assembled episode. These are production findings, **not** canonical
rules. Promote them only after repeated production validation.

## PE-003-01 --- Multiple complementary shots outperform one long cinematic shot

**Observation:** One continuous 10--15 second AI shot feels slower than
several complementary shots covering the same narrative beat.
**Recommendation:** Prefer 2--3 complementary shots per narrative beat
where production cost is reasonable.

## PE-003-02 --- Documentary evidence is a primary trust signal

**Observation:** Real reports and reputable publications substantially
increase perceived credibility. **Recommendation:** Continue using
documentary artifacts as a core visual pillar.

## PE-003-03 --- Mechanism diagrams should resolve quickly

**Observation:** The mechanism is understood before the animation
completes. **Recommendation:** Keep dedicated diagram sequences around
5--6 seconds before returning to live footage.

## PE-003-04 --- Final synchronization belongs in assembly

**Observation:** Small timing adjustments during assembly produced a
better result than reopening approved upstream artifacts.
**Recommendation:** Prefer assembly-level pacing adjustments once
upstream artifacts are locked.

## Candidate for Future Validation

Future mechanism diagrams may integrate with documentary footage instead
of occupying the full frame continuously. Validate across multiple
published episodes before promoting this into the Visual Identity
System.

## Next Review Trigger

Review these findings after 3--5 published episodes. Promote only those
repeatedly confirmed in production.
