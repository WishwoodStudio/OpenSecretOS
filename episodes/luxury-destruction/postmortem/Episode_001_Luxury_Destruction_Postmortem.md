# Episode 1 Editorial Postmortem --- Luxury Destruction

**Status:** Postmortem (not canonical)

## Executive Summary

This document captures editorial lessons from the first published Open
Secret episode. Treat every finding as provisional until validated
across multiple episodes.

## 1. Visual cadence matters more than visual beauty

A single beautiful 10--15 second AI shot with only a slow push-in feels
less engaging than several related shots.

**Working rule** - Build every \~15-second AI block from 2--3 connected
shots. - Increase cadence without sacrificing the Documentary Hybrid
style.

## 2. Prefer living environments over empty environments

The objective is not simply to look expensive.

The objective is to look alive.

Prefer people, offices, factories, logistics, traffic and visible
activity.

Reduce dependence on empty interiors, empty warehouses and static
shelves.

Premium should come from believable activity, not stillness.

## 3. Real documents performed extremely well

Authentic reports and evidence graphics strengthened trust and broke up
cinematic footage naturally.

## 4. Mechanism diagrams deserve premium treatment

Future diagrams should prioritize cinematic presentation and rapid
comprehension.

Aim for roughly 5--6 seconds unless later evidence suggests otherwise.

## 5. Human-assisted evidence sourcing

Successful workflow:

Claude -\> evidence request -\> human retrieves sources -\> ChatGPT
prepares polished graphics -\> Claude assembles.

## 6. Automate repetition, not difficulty

Do not automate tasks simply because they can be automated.

Automate tasks that repeatedly save production time.

## 7. Team strengths

Claude: research, scripting, directing, assembly.

ChatGPT: strategy, editorial review, premium visual artifacts.

Human: quick physical tasks that are cheaper than automation.
