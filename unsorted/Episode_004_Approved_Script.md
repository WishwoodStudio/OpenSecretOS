# Open Secret — Episode 004

## Approved Script (Canonical)

**Status:** APPROVED  
**Language:** English  
**Version:** Final

\---

Why don't governments just print more money?



They say they don't have enough. They raise taxes. They cut spending.



But here's the strange part. Money isn't wealth.



It's a receipt. For a house someone built. For food someone grew. For an hour of someone's work.



You can print more receipts. But you can't print another house. You can't print more food.



More receipts just chase the same real things.



Printing money doesn't create wealth. It just cuts the same pie into more slices.



Your slice gets smaller. And they call it inflation.



So next time they say "we need to print more" — ask yourself: whose slice are they taking?
